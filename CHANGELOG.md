# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-09-09

Public GitHub release aligned with the final SCED-oriented manuscript.

- Standardized the research term from `useownership` to `usownership` in the public API, configurations, result headers and documentation.
- Added `Parameters.start_year = 2027` and `calendar_year` to simulation trajectories.
- Mapped model year 10 to 2037 and model year 50 to 2077 without changing economic dynamics.
- Bundled the 2027-2037 SCED manuscript and online supplement.
- Preserved the verified 549-path numerical results; only terminology and calendar labels were transformed.
- Added release-regression tests and publication-facing documentation.
- Kept the distinction between the 2027-2037 institutional design window and the 50-year 2027-2077 structural horizon.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-09-08

- Added strengthened fiscal counterfactuals, matched dividend levies and targeted hybrid policy.
- Expanded experiment to 549 macro paths.
- Added policy-comparison outputs, figures, workbook and tests.
