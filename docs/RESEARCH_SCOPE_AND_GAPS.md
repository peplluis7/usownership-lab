# Evidence and inference boundary

## Supported by executed accounting and scenarios

Share conservation; monetary and goods identities; exact matching of the specified dividend-levy and equity cash contracts; compound dilution; present-value arithmetic; conditional transfer and book-wealth trajectories; adverse coverage and liquidation cases.

## Supported by external sources, not inferred from the simulations

The historical precedents for capital funds, data remuneration and equity-based AI taxes; conditional AI-growth theories and separate observed productivity effects; current EU fiscal shares; monetary-financing legal constraints; general Elsevier preparation guidance. See the entry-by-entry source ledger for bibliographic details and access limits.

## Not yet empirically established

Whether user ownership raises useful participation beyond matched cash; whether it is more durable than a statutory transfer; whether governance rights create net social gains; how mandatory awards affect financing, entry and innovation; real contribution-scoring costs and exclusion; demand for sovereign money after reform; transitional banking and open-economy responses.

## Priority empirical work

An equity-versus-matched-cash-versus-financial-asset experiment, with pre-specified coverage and outcome measures. Then estimate the investment and commitment margins and integrate household microdata, bank credit, public debt and international substitution before national policy claims.
